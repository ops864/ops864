# Probative Growth

Marketing agency for US law firms: SEO and ads, with monthly prices published on the site.

We also publish open data on legal marketing and keep it current.

- **Data:** [legal-marketing-data](https://github.com/ops864/legal-marketing-data) — 95 datasets on attorney advertising rules, markets and costs. CC BY 4.0, each with a DOI and the date it was last checked at source.
- **Browse the data:** https://probativegrowth.com/data/
- **MCP server:** `legal-marketing-data` and `legal-markets` in the MCP Registry, for AI assistants that need to look up the rules and market figures.
- **Site:** https://probativegrowth.com (English and Spanish)

Nothing here is legal advice. Check the rule at its source before relying on it.
